# Key dates

These are the **planned** dates. If anything moves, we'll update this page and tell you in the next news update. Your department's wave is confirmed with your manager before it starts.

## Microsoft 365 (office staff)

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| November to December 2026 | Getting ready: planning, licences and choosing a migration partner |
| January to February 2027 | Building and testing our new Microsoft 365 setup |
| March 2027 | Pilot group moves first (about 10 people, including our champions) |
| April 2027 | Wave 1 moves (about 30 people) |
| May 2027 | Wave 2 moves (about 30 people) |
| June 2027 | Wave 3 moves. Everyone is on Microsoft 365 by **14 June 2027** |
| June to August 2027 | Extra help on hand while everyone settles in |
| **27 August 2027** | Google accounts close |

**Drivers and trucking staff:** you don't move to Microsoft 365. Our driver app and website carry on as normal. If you have a Google account through work, we'll tell you well before it closes.

## ISO 27001 (everyone)

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| 2027 | We check where we stand and fix any gaps, much of it built into Microsoft 365 |
| From September 2027 | Security awareness training, policies and internal checks |
| **May 2028** | Final certification audit |

## What you'll get along the way

- An update here every fortnight
- Training at your site, in small groups
- A champion in your team you can ask
- Help clinics and "do it with me" sessions
- A printed quick reference card
