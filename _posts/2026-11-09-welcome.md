Over the next 18 months, Coho Group is making three changes to how we use technology. This page is where we'll keep you up to date.

## The three changes

1. **Moving to Microsoft 365.** Office staff will move from Google (Gmail, Drive) to Microsoft (Outlook, OneDrive, Teams). Drivers and trucking staff don't move. Our driver app and website carry on as normal.
2. **Working towards ISO 27001.** This is an international standard that shows a business looks after information properly. More of our customers are asking for it.
3. **Simple rules for new technology.** Knowing what systems we use, and checking before signing up to new ones.

## What this means for you right now

Nothing changes yet. We're in the planning stage. Before anything affects you, you'll hear about it here, from your manager and from your site champion.

## How you'll hear from us

- **This page**, updated every fortnight
- **Email, noticeboards, toolbox talks** and the driver app
- **Your champion**: a person in your team who'll be trained first and can help you

## Nobody is left to work it out alone

We know not everyone is confident with technology, and that's fine. There will be training at your site, help sessions, and people on hand on the day you switch over.

And remember: **nobody from Coho Group will ever ask for your password.**
